Questions

How do I pay?

When you choose a plan you are sent to a secure payment page run by our payment provider. We never see or store your card details on this site.

What are my Member ID and call number?

After you join you get a Member ID (like EO-1234567) and a 9 digit call number. Share your call number with other members. When they enter it in Eco Orbit Meetings, your device rings and you can talk with video and voice.

Can I use my numbers on another device?

Yes. Choose "Sign in with my numbers" and enter your name, Member ID and call number. You will be reachable on that device whenever the Meetings page is open.

Can I switch plans later?

Yes. You can move up or down whenever you like, and the change applies from your next billing period. Your numbers stay the same.

What does the yearly option save?

Paying yearly gives you two months free compared with paying month by month.

Do I need to be a member to use the site?

No. The Explorer plan is free and lets you try the core tools, including the 3D simulator and the cyber security test. Member calling needs a paid plan.
