EcoOrbit keeps growing with every new member

The chart appears when the first members register.

Every account created on EcoOrbit and every Member ID issued on the Membership page is added to this chart automatically, month by month, so you can watch the community grow.

EcoOrbit began as an idea for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026: make orbital sustainability easy to explore. Today it brings a live globe, a Sustainability Score, collision risk alerts, carbon credit tracking, a kids’ learning hub and member calling together in one place.

EcoOrbit is an independent participant project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA. All figures are shown for the community on this site.
