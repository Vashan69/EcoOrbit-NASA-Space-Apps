Overview

This project is a static website. It has no user accounts, no sign-up form, no advertising and no analytics or tracking scripts. It shows satellite, debris and sustainability information for education and demonstration.

The only information kept is what you choose to save in your own browser, like your theme or checklist progress. It never leaves your device, and you can delete it any time from the controls below.

What we collect

Short version: nothing about you is sent to us. Here is the detail.

Kept on your device only

  • Theme choice (dark or light), if you allow it
  • Your safety checklist progress, if you allow it
  • A small record of your privacy choices
  • Your voice guide choices (on/off, voice and speed)
  • The name on your test certificate, and your video likes and comments

Never collected

  • Name, email, phone number or location
  • Passwords or payment details
  • Browsing history or advertising profiles
  • Anything from your camera, microphone or files

Third-party services

To display fonts, icons and the 3D globe, pages load code from public content delivery networks. Like any website, those providers can see your IP address and browser type when your browser requests files from them.

Fonts (Google Fonts)
Used to display the typefaces. Google serves the font files and receives a standard web request. See Google's privacy policy for how it handles those requests.
Code libraries (Tailwind CSS, Font Awesome, Three.js)
Loaded from public CDNs so the design, icons and 3D globe work. No personal information is added to these requests by this project.
Orbital and space data (NASA, CelesTrak)
Satellite and debris figures come from or are modelled on public space-agency datasets. Nothing about you is sent when viewing them.
Payments (Membership)
If you buy a membership, you are sent to a secure payment page run by our payment provider. That provider collects your payment details, not this site, and its own privacy policy applies.
Chat assistant
The chat assistant runs in your browser and your messages are not saved. If it cannot answer a general-knowledge question itself, it looks the topic up on Wikipedia, which means the words of that question are sent to Wikipedia. Its own privacy policy applies there.
Links to other websites
If you follow a link off this site, that site's own privacy policy applies. Check the address in your browser before you type anything.
