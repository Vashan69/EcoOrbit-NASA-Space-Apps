About EcoOrbit

EcoOrbit is a gateway to orbital sustainability, helping the next generation of explorers understand and protect the space around Earth. Built as The Third World Peace VLZO UFO, it brings satellites, space debris, launch carbon impact and responsible orbital operations together in one space-themed experience that anyone can explore without specialist software.

EcoOrbit is created by a six-person team from Bangladesh for the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026 under the theme “The Next Frontier.” It is an independent participant project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.

The platform is designed for learning and demonstration. Risk and sustainability outputs are educational estimates, not real flight guidance. Want to meet the people behind it? Meet the team here.

EcoOrbit Values
  • Education first: clear, friendly explanations for students, kids and curious adults.
  • Sustainability: measuring the footprint of launches, satellites and debris so that orbit stays usable for everyone.
  • Accessibility: works in the browser, with a voice guide and an astronaut helper to support every visitor.
  • Openness and honesty: our outputs are clearly labelled as educational estimates.
What is EcoOrbit?

EcoOrbit is an educational space-technology concept focused on orbital sustainability, satellite and debris awareness, carbon impact, and accessible tools for exploring the next frontier. Its motto is Learn • Explore • Model • Protect.

Visitors can observe satellites and debris on an interactive globe, understand them through calculators and explainers, and imagine future solutions such as green credits for responsible orbital operations.

What can I do on EcoOrbit?
  • Explore the 3D VLZO UFO Simulator and the live orbital globe
  • Calculate a Sustainability Score and view collision risk alerts
  • Track carbon credits for orbital activity
  • Take the cyber security test and earn a certificate
  • Watch VLZO TV and use the voice guide
  • Let kids learn in the Junior Space Explorers Hub and Kids Story
  • Call other members in EcoOrbit Meetings

How can I get involved with EcoOrbit?

Create a free account with Sign Up on the EcoOrbit home page, then explore the tools. For member calling, a Member ID and more features, choose a plan on the Membership page. The free Explorer plan lets you try the core tools.

How can I connect with the EcoOrbit community?

Sign up, join the Members directory and use EcoOrbit Meetings to talk with other members by voice and video. Follow #EcoOrbit to see project updates.

Is EcoOrbit part of NASA?

No. EcoOrbit is built for the NASA Space Apps Challenge, a global hackathon where participants use space and Earth science data, technology and creative problem-solving. Our project is presented as an independent participant project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
