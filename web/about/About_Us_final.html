About the project

Why we built it

The project brings several ideas together into one space-themed experience so visitors can learn about orbital sustainability without needing specialist software or mission-control experience.

🛰️

Orbital awareness

Interactive satellite and debris experiences help explain orbital objects, their movement, and why congestion and debris matter.

🌱

Sustainability

Our sustainability tools explore launch footprint, de-orbit planning, debris mitigation, and the idea of measuring environmental impact in space operations.

🧠

Education first

The platform is designed for learning and demonstration. Risk and sustainability outputs are educational estimates, not real flight guidance.

What we are exploring

More about our mission

Our work connects the project's existing tools and pages into a single story about responsible activity in low Earth orbit.

01 • Observe

Explore satellite and debris information through the interactive globe and telemetry experiences.

02 • Understand

Use the calculators, explanations and educational pages to connect orbital activity with sustainability and safety.

03 • Imagine

Use the concept of green credits and responsible orbital operations to think about possible future solutions.

About NASA Space Apps Challenge

NASA Space Apps Challenge is a global hackathon-style event where participants use space and Earth science data, technology and creative problem-solving to develop ideas and prototypes. Our project is being prepared for the 2026 challenge and is presented as an independent participant project; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by NASA.
