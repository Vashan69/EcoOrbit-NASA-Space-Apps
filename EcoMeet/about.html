The people behind EcoMeet

Meet the founder and advisor who guide our mission of simple, lightweight and accessible video meetings.

Founder & Advisor
Founder

Vashan Mozumder

Founder, EcoMeet

Vashan Mozumder is the founder of EcoMeet. He created EcoMeet as a lightweight way to meet online: video meetings with chat, hand raising, screen share and call by number, all peer to peer with no downloads, just a link.

He also brought NASA open data into the experience, so people can explore live space information while they meet, and he is part of the NASA Space Apps Challenge 2026.

Advisor

Honorable Nurul Hai

Managing Director & CEO, Startup Bangladesh Limited (SBL)

Nurul Hai is a Bangladeshi venture capitalist, impact fund manager, and corporate leader. He serves as the Managing Director and CEO of Startup Bangladesh Limited (SBL), the government’s flagship venture capital firm under the ICT Division.
